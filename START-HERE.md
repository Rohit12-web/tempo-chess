# Open TempoChess

1. Extract the complete project ZIP and open the folder containing `landing.html`.
2. Open `landing.html` in a modern browser, then create or choose a local profile.
3. From Home, choose Play, Puzzles, Openings, Notebook, Progress or Learn.
4. Expand **Board & sound** to enable move sounds, adjust the volume or choose a high-contrast board.

## Explore the features

- Play against the bot or a friend on the same device.
- Save positions from Puzzles or Replay to your personal Notebook, where you can search, edit and delete notes.
- Use **Practice mistakes** to revisit puzzles where you needed help.
- Finish an opening lesson and choose **Play from here** to continue against the bot.
- Open Learn for five interactive beginner rule exercises, or Progress to view your practice statistics.

## Keep your progress

Keep `css/`, `js/` and `assets/` beside the HTML pages. No installation, backend or build step is required.

Profiles and progress are saved in your browser. Use the same browser and project location to retain access to them; storage for directly opened HTML files varies between browsers. Clearing browser data or deleting a profile removes its saved progress. Local profiles do not sync across devices.
